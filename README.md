# Mask-Conditioned Recoloring of Urban SAR Satellite Images

CS 131 final project, Stanford, Spring 2026 — Sukrut Oak & Abel John

A structure-preserving recoloring pipeline for urban SAR imagery on the SEN12MS-CR Bay Area dataset. Builds on our prior CS 231N work ([Improved Recoloring of SAR Satellite Images](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/2024/papers/improved-recoloring-of-sar-satellite-images.pdf)).
